    How it works

    From you, to them, and back.

    1. You make it theirs

      Choose what you call them, add their name, pick a colour and write a few words from the heart. It only takes a couple of minutes, and you’ll see it come together as you go.

    2. We print it, just for them

      Your book is made to order, printed within a few days and sent by Priority post to the address you choose. While it’s on its way, we’ll send you a printable card, so you have something lovely to hand them straight away.

    3. They fill it in, then give it back

      There’s no rush and no right order. They can answer one page tonight and another next year. When it finally comes back to you, it holds their stories, in their own handwriting.

    Questions

    The ones people ask us.

    How long does it take to arrive?

    Each book is printed to order, which takes a few days, then sent by Priority shipping. In New Zealand, allow about three to four weeks from order to door. Other countries are usually quicker.

    What if they don’t fill it all in?

    They don’t need to. Every question is optional, and some pages are meant to be skipped. It doesn’t need to be finished to be precious.

    We’re not that close. Is this still for us?

    The book opens by saying that not every family is close, simple or easy. The questions start light, and the deeper ones are marked so they can be left for later, or for never.

    Can I change the spelling of Mum?

    Yes. The cover uses whatever you call them: Mum, Mom, Mama, Mam, Dad or your own word. US orders use American spelling throughout the book.

    Can I return it?

    Because every book is personalised, we can’t take returns for a change of mind. If there’s a printing fault, we’ll have it reprinted at no cost.

    Is my message printed in the book?

    Yes. Your message, up to 400 characters, is printed on its own page at the front, with your name.
